Fast, effective workouts

Two or three full-body strength sessions per week, depending on the rota, with short optional movement on other days. Choose home or gym.

Warm up for 3–5 minutes. Choose a resistance that leaves about 2 reps in reserve, and increase gradually when all sets feel controlled. Stop if an exercise causes sharp pain, unusual numbness or weakness.
